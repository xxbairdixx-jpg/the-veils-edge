# Epic 20-Session Campaign Foundation

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign Title:** The Veil's Edge  
**Theme:** A world on the brink of collapse, where ancient forces awaken and heroes must navigate political intrigue, supernatural threats, and cosmic consequences to prevent a cataclysmic event.

**Overarching Main Objective:** The party must uncover the source of the "Veil" - an otherworldly barrier weakening reality - and stop its destruction before it shatters the world into chaos. They must also confront the entity behind this destruction: the Final BBEG.

**Final BBEG (Level 18-20):**
- **Name:** The Weaving Mind
- **Race/Type:** A cosmic entity of pure thought, existing outside conventional reality
- **Motivation:** To reshape existence according to its own chaotic will, believing current reality is flawed and needs "reweaving"
- **Backstory:** Once a benevolent creator god, the Weaving Mind became corrupted by the power it wielded. Its attempts at "improvement" began as subtle changes but escalated into systematic destruction of planetary systems.
- **Connection to everything:** The Weaving Mind is the ultimate source of all threats in the campaign - local cults worshiping it, regional tyrants serving its will, continental powers being manipulated by its agents, and even planar beings being drawn toward its influence. Its power manifests through the "Veil" that's tearing reality apart.

## 2. Mini-Arc BBEGs

**BBEG 1 (Sessions 1-5): Local Threat**
- **Name:** The Iron Hand
- **Race/Type:** Human Warlord, former knight commander
- **Motivation:** To establish a tyrannical rule over the region and create an army to serve his ultimate goal of becoming a regional power
- **Connection to Final BBEG:** The Iron Hand is a pawn of the Weaving Mind's local agents. He believes he's serving a higher purpose but is actually being used as a tool for the cosmic entity's plan.
- **Lair Location:** Fortress of the Iron Crown, atop Mount Gorm

**BBEG 2 (Sessions 6-10): Regional Threat**
- **Name:** The Whispering Queen
- **Race/Type:** Elven sorceress, leader of the Shadow Syndicate
- **Motivation:** To expand her influence across the region and establish a network of shadowy operations that can be leveraged for her own power
- **Connection to Final BBEG:** The Whispering Queen is one of several "weavers" created by the Weaving Mind. She believes she's working toward a greater good but is actually being used as a local agent for the cosmic entity.
- **Lair Location:** The Shadow Keep, beneath the city of Luminara

**BBEG 3 (Sessions 11-15): Continental Threat**
- **Name:** The Iron Crown Emperor
- **Race/Type:** Human emperor, former warlord who rose to power through military might
- **Motivation:** To consolidate his empire and create a powerful state that can eventually challenge the Weaving Mind's influence
- **Connection to Final BBEG:** The Emperor is being manipulated by the Weaving Mind's continental agents. He believes he's building an empire for humanity but is actually being used as a pawn in the cosmic entity's plan.
- **Lair Location:** Imperial Palace, capital of the Empire

**BBEG 4 (Sessions 16-19): Epic/Planar Threat**
- **Name:** The Planar Archon
- **Race/Type:** Demigod-like being from another plane, now bound to this world
- **Motivation:** To establish a foothold in reality and serve the Weaving Mind's will by controlling planar forces
- **Connection to Final BBEG:** The Planar Archon is one of several entities created directly by the Weaving Mind. It believes it's serving an ancient cosmic purpose but is actually being used as a direct agent for the entity.
- **Lair Location:** The Planar Nexus, a dimensional gateway in the mountains

## 3. World Map

**World Name:** Terminus Prime
**Regions:**
1. **Northern Fjords (Arctic biome):** Cold, harsh environment with ancient ruins and ice-worshipping tribes
2. **Central Plains (Temperate biome):** Agricultural heartland with diverse cultures and the main campaign region
3. **Southern Wastes (Desert biome):** Arid, dangerous territory with nomadic tribes and ancient ruins

**Major Cities:**
1. **Luminara:** Elven city in the Central Plains, population 45,000, democratic council government, known for its magical arts and trade
2. **Ironhold:** Human fortress-city on Mount Gorm, population 28,000, military oligarchy, known for its steel production and martial prowess
3. **Imperial Capital:** Capital of the Empire in the Southern Wastes, population 120,000, imperial bureaucracy, known for its grand architecture and political intrigue

**Dungeon/Ruin Locations:**
1. **The Iron Crown Fortress (Mount Gorm):** Fortress of BBEG 1
2. **The Shadow Keep (Luminara):** Lair of BBEG 2  
3. **Imperial Palace:** Lair of BBEG 3
4. **Planar Nexus (Southern Mountains):** Lair of BBEG 4
5. **Fjord Ruins:** Ancient elven ruins in the Northern Fjords
6. **Desert Oasis Temple:** Ancient ruin in the Southern Wastes
7. **Central Plains Monastery:** Ancient religious site

## 4. Pantheon

1. **The Weaving Mind (Final BBEG):** Domain of Thought, Chaotic Evil, Symbol: A tangled web of threads, Relationship to BBEGs: Creator/Source
2. **The Weaver's Hand:** Domain of War, Lawful Neutral, Symbol: Iron gauntlet, Relationship to BBEG 1: Direct agent
3. **The Whispering Voice:** Domain of Magic, Chaotic Good (corrupted), Symbol: Crystal orb, Relationship to BBEG 2: Creator/Source
4. **The Planar Archon:** Domain of Evil, Lawful Evil, Symbol: Demonic sigil, Relationship to BBEG 4: Direct entity
5. **The Iron Crown Emperor:** Domain of War, Lawful Good (corrupted), Symbol: Imperial crown, Relationship to BBEG 3: Creator/Source

## 5. Factions

1. **Shadow Syndicate:** Leader - The Whispering Queen, Goals - Expand shadowy operations across the region, Relationship to party - Hostile
2. **Iron Guard:** Leader - General of Ironhold, Goals - Maintain order and security in the fortress-city, Relationship to party - Neutral/Hostile if crossed
3. **Imperial Court:** Leader - High Chancellor, Goals - Preserve imperial power and stability, Relationship to party - Hostile unless proven otherwise
