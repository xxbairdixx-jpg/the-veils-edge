# The Veil's Edge - NPC Reference Guide

## Introduction
This comprehensive reference guide contains detailed information about all NPCs appearing in the "The Veil's Edge" D&D 5e campaign on Terminus Prime. The guide includes basic information, physical descriptions, personality traits, dialogue examples, and game statistics for each character.

## NPC List

### Session 1: The Awakening
**Eldor the Village Elder**
- **Basic Info**: Human male, 45 years old; Role: Village Elder; Location: Sunward village; Sessions: 1-5
- **Physical Description**: Fair skin, brown hair with streaks of gray, blue eyes, 5'11"
- **Personality**: 
  - Traits: Wise, concerned, protective
  - Ideal: Community protection
  - Bond: Family lives in the village; wants to protect his people
  - Flaw: Overly cautious, sometimes suspicious
- **Voice Notes**: Warm and gentle but with a hint of worry
- **Dialogue**:
  - Friendly greeting: "My children, what brings you here? Strange lights have been appearing in our fields at night. Something is wrong."
  - About main quest: "The strange lights are not natural phenomena but something supernatural."
  - Intimidated/threatened: "Please don't hurt my people! We need to understand this before it's too late!"
- **Game Info**: Neutral ally; Alignment: Lawful Good

**Mara the Blacksmith**
- **Basic Info**: Human female, 35 years old; Role: Blacksmith; Location: Sunward village; Sessions: 1-5
- **Physical Description**: Olive skin, short black hair, brown eyes, 5'6"
- **Personality**: 
  - Traits: Practical, no-nonsense, skilled
  - Ideal: Skill and craftsmanship
  - Bond: Family runs the blacksmith shop; wants to provide for her family
  - Flaw: Can be impatient with beginners
- **Voice Notes**: Firm but helpful
- **Dialogue**:
  - Friendly greeting: "What's going on? The goblins have been raiding our fields recently."
  - About main quest: "We need help defending against them. I can make better weapons for you."
  - Intimidated/threatened: "Don't hurt my family! We'll fight back!"
- **Game Info**: Neutral ally; Alignment: Lawful Good

**Kael the Young Farmer**
- **Basic Info**: Human male, 25 years old; Role: Young farmer; Location: Sunward village; Sessions: 1-5
- **Physical Description**: Fair skin, blonde hair, green eyes, 5'9"
- **Personality**: 
  - Traits: Energetic, curious, optimistic
  - Ideal: Growth and prosperity
  - Bond: Family farm; wants to expand his family's land
  - Flaw: Can be impulsive
- **Voice Notes**: Cheerful and enthusiastic
- **Dialogue**:
  - Friendly greeting: "I saw those lights last night! They were moving in patterns I've never seen before!"
  - About main quest: "Tell me more about what you saw. Can we help protect our farm?"
  - Intimidated/threatened: "The goblins are coming! We need to fight back!"
- **Game Info**: Neutral ally; Alignment: Lawful Good
