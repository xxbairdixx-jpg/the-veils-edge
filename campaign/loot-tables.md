## 3. Magic Items – Tiered by Rarity
### Common (≈10 items)
- **Potion of Healing** – restores 2d4+2 hp.
- **Wand of Light** – one use per day, light source.
- **Rope of Climbing** – 50 ft, never frays.
- **Cloak of Protection** – +1 AC and saving throws.
- **Bag of Tricks (common)** – pull a common animal.
- **Decanter of Endless Water** – 10 gallons per day.
- **Pearl of Power** – regain one expended spell slot.
- **Gloves of Thievery** – +5 to Sleight of Hand.
- **Lantern of Revealing** – reveals invisible creatures.
- **Potion of Climbing** – grants climbing speed 30 ft.

### Uncommon (≈8 items)
- **Cloak of Elvenkind** – stealth advantage in forest.
- **Ring of Evasion** – once per long rest, halve damage on a successful Dex save.
- **Boots of Speed** – double movement for 10 min.
- **Wand of Magic Missiles** – 3 charges, 1d4+1 each.
- **Scroll (Uncommon Spell)** – e.g., *Cure Wounds* (2nd level).
- **Amulet of Health** – sets Con to 19.
- **Pearl of Power** – regain two expended spell slots.
- **Potion of Fire Breath** – 1d6 fire damage, 3 uses.

### Rare (≈5 items)
- **+1 Weapon** – adds +1 to attack and damage.
- **Ring of Protection** – +1 AC and saving throws.
- **Boots of the Winterlands** – resistance to cold.
- **Wand of Polymorph** – 3 charges, *Polymorph* spell.
- **Scroll (Rare Spell)** – e.g., *Teleportation Circle*.

### Very Rare (≈3 items)
- **Amulet of the Planes** – once per day, *Plane Shift*.
- **Bag of Holding** – extra 64 cu ft capacity.
- **Potion of Invisibility** – 1d4 rounds.

### Legendary (≈2 items)
- **Staff of Power** – +2 to attack and damage; multiple spell effects.
- **Ring of Three Wishes** – three wishes, last wish has a cost.

## 4. Treasure Maps & Scrolls
| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Map to the Hidden Vault | Leads to a buried treasure chest with 5,000 gp and a *Cloak of Protection*.
| Map to the Crystal Cave | Reveals a cavern full of 10 gems (rare).
| Scroll of *Find Familiar* | Free spell for characters who need an ally.

## 5. Wondrous Items
- **Tome of Clear Thought** – increases Intelligence by 2.
- **Cloak of the Manta Ray** – swim speed 60 ft, breathe underwater.
- **Boots of Levitation** – levitate 10 ft per round.
- **Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location** – immune to divination spells.
