# Session 2: The Shadow's Approach

## Session Title and Summary
The players investigate the strange lights further, discovering they're connected to a nearby cave system. They must explore this cave while dealing with increasing threats from the goblins.

## Session Objectives
- Explore the cave system near Sunward village
- Discover what causes the strange lights
- Defend against multiple waves of goblin attacks

## Detailed Encounter Descriptions

**Encounter 1: The Cave Entrance**
The players enter the cave system, finding it filled with strange glowing fungi that emit light. They must navigate through this environment.

**Enemies:**

  AC: 13
