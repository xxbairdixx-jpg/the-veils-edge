# Session 8: The Shadow's Alliance

## Session Title and Summary
The players continue their investigation into the shadow cult, discovering that they've formed alliances with other dark forces. They must confront this while dealing with more powerful enemies.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the shadow cult's alliances
- Discover what they're trying to achieve
- Defend against powerful cultists
