# Session 1: The Awakening

## Session Title and Summary
The players awaken in a small village near the edge of the Sunlit Plains, discovering that strange occurrences have been happening in their homeland. They must investigate these events while dealing with local threats.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate strange lights appearing at night
- Meet with the village elder for information
- Defend the village from an approaching threat

**NPCs:**

1. **Eldor the Village Elder**
   - Race: Human, skin color: fair, height: 5'11", hair: brown with streaks of gray, eye color: blue
   - Personality Traits: Wise, concerned, protective
   - Ideals: Community protection
   - Bonds: Family lives in the village; wants to protect his people
   - Flaws: Overly cautious, sometimes suspicious
   - Voice/Speech Notes: Warm and gentle but with a hint of worry

2. **Mara the Blacksmith**
   - Race: Human, skin color: olive, height: 5'6", hair: short black, eye color: brown
   - Personality Traits: Practical, no-nonsense, skilled
   - Ideals: Skill and craftsmanship
   - Bonds: Family runs the blacksmith shop; wants to provide for her family
   - Flaws: Can be impatient with beginners
   - Voice/Speech Notes: Firm but helpful

3. **Kael the Young Farmer**
   - Race: Human, skin color: fair, height: 5'9", hair: blonde, eye color: green
   - Personality Traits: Energetic, curious, optimistic
   - Ideals: Growth and prosperity
   - Bonds: Family farm; wants to expand his family's land
   - Flaws: Can be impulsive
   - Voice/Speech Notes: Cheerful and enthusiastic

**Encounter 1: Strange Lights in the Fields**
The players investigate strange lights appearing at night in the fields surrounding Sunward village. They discover that these are not natural phenomena but something supernatural.

**Enemies:**

- **Glowing Fungi (3)** - Level 1 creatures
  HP: 10 each
  AC: 12
  Speed: 30 ft.
  Ability Scores: STR 8, DEX 14, CON 10, INT 6, WIS 11, CHA 9
  Saving Throws: DEX +4, WIS +3
  Skills: Perception +5, Stealth +7
  Special Abilities: Bioluminescence (can emit light)
  
- **Shadowy Figure** - Level 1 creature
  HP: 20
  AC: 13
  Speed: 30 ft.
  Ability Scores: STR 12, DEX 10, CON 11, INT 8, WIS 9, CHA 6
  Saving Throws: STR +2, DEX +0, CON +1
  Skills: Perception +3, Stealth +5
  Special Abilities: Invisibility (can become invisible)

**Encounter 2: The Attack**
As the players investigate further, they are attacked by a group of goblins who have been attracted to the strange lights.

**Enemies:**

- **Goblin (4)** - Level 1 creatures
  HP: 8 each
  AC: 12
  Speed: 25 ft.
  Ability Scores: STR 10, DEX 14, CON 10, INT 6, WIS 9, CHA 5
  Saving Throws: DEX +4, CON +2
  Skills: Perception +3, Stealth +5
  Special Abilities: Can cast "Dancing Lights" once per day

- **Goblin Chief** - Level 1 creature (leader)
  HP: 20
  AC: 13
  Speed: 25 ft.
  Ability Scores: STR 12, DEX 12, CON 11, INT 8, WIS 10, CHA 6
  Saving Throws: STR +2, DEX +2, CON +1
  Skills: Perception +4, Stealth +5
  Special Abilities: Can cast "Goblin's Greed" once per day

**Treasure and Rewards**

- **Common Items**: 3d6 gold pieces (approximately 15 gp), a small iron dagger (+1), a leather tunic, a pair of leather gloves.
  
- **Side Quests**:
  - **Investigate the Strange Lights**: Find out what causes the strange lights in the fields
  - **Protect the Village**: Help defend Sunward village from further attacks
