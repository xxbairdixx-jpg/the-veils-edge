# Session 12: The Void's Alliance

## Session Title and Summary
The players continue their investigation into the Void Nomads, discovering that they've formed alliances with other dark forces. They must confront this while dealing with more powerful enemies.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the Void Nomads' alliances
- Discover what they're trying to achieve
- Defend against powerful nomadic raiders

4. **Kaelor the Nomad Leader** (Level 13)
   - Race: Human, skin color: pale white with grayish tinge, height: 6'5", hair: long white, eye color: yellow
   - Personality Traits: Calm, authoritative, mysterious
   - Ideals: Survival and adaptation to the void
   - Bonds: The nomadic tribes; wants to protect his people from the void's dangers
   - Flaws: Can be cold, believes in survival of the fittest
   - Voice/Speech Notes: Deep, resonating voice with a hint of ancient wisdom

5. **Void Shaman** (Level 13)
   - Race: Human, skin color: pale white with grayish tinge, height: 6'0", hair: long white, eye color: yellow
   - Personality Traits: Calm, controlled, dangerous
   - Ideals: Void adaptation and survival
   - Bonds: Kaelor's goals; wants to maintain the nomadic structure
   - Flaws: Rigid in his beliefs about void survival, willing to sacrifice others for rituals
   - Voice/Speech Notes: Soft but commanding voice

6. **Void Lord** (Level 13)
   - Race: Tiefling, skin color: dark purple with grayish tinge, height: 5'10", hair: long black with purple highlights, eye color: red
   - Personality Traits: Ambitious, cunning, mysterious
   - Ideals: Power and influence through void
   - Bonds: The Void Nomads' goals; wants to expand his influence
   - Flaws: Selfish, willing to betray others for power
   - Voice/Speech Notes: Smooth but with a hint of menace
