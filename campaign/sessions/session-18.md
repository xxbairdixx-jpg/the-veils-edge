# Session 18: The Final Confrontation

## Session Title and Summary
The players have successfully dealt with the main villain, but they must face their ultimate challenge - the final confrontation that will determine the fate of Aethelgard. They must confront this final threat.

## Session Objectives
- Face the main villain in their true form
- Discover their ultimate plan for the world
- Defend against the ultimate threat
