# Session 6: The Cult's Stronghold

## Session Title and Summary
The players investigate the shadow cult's activities, discovering their main stronghold deep within the cave system. They must infiltrate this stronghold while dealing with more powerful enemies.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the shadow cult's main stronghold
- Discover what they're doing there
- Defend against powerful cultists

## Detailed Encounter Descriptions
