# Session 14: The Planar Invasion

## Session Title and Summary
The players investigate the planar connection, discovering that entities from another plane are invading our world. They must confront these beings while dealing with powerful planar threats.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the planar invasion
- Discover what these entities are doing in our world
- Defend against powerful planar beings

5. **Planar Entity** (Level 15)
   - Race: Celestial, skin color: radiant white, height: 7'0", hair: flowing silver, eye color: golden

   - Ideals: Planar balance and order
   - Bonds: The plane's goals; wants to maintain planar harmony
   - Flaws: Can be distant, believes in strict planar laws
   - Voice/Speech Notes: Soft but commanding voice with a hint of ancient wisdom

6. **Planar Invader** (Level 15)
   - Race: Shadow Demon, skin color: dark gray, height: 6'2", hair: flowing black, eye color: red

   - Ideals: Chaos and destruction
   - Bonds: The invasion's goals; wants to destroy the plane
   - Flaws: Ruthless, believes in total domination
