# Session 9: The Void Nomads' Approach

## Session Title and Summary
The players have successfully dealt with the shadow cult, but a new threat emerges - the Void Nomads. These nomadic tribes have appeared in the region, seeking to exploit the void's power.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the arrival of the Void Nomads
- Discover their connection to the strange lights
- Defend against these nomadic raiders
