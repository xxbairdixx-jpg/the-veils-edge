# Session 16: The Final Confrontation

## Session Title and Summary
The players have successfully dealt with the planar alliance, but they must face their ultimate challenge - the main villain who has been orchestrating all these events. They must confront this final threat.

## Session Objectives
- Face the main villain
- Discover their true identity and motives
- Defend against the ultimate threat
