# Session 3: The Whispering Chamber

## Session Title and Summary
The players continue exploring the cave system, discovering a chamber where strange whispers are coming from. They must investigate this chamber while dealing with more advanced threats.

## Session Objectives
- Explore the deeper parts of the cave system
- Investigate the source of the mysterious whispers
- Defend against multiple waves of goblin attacks

## Detailed Encounter Descriptions
