# Session 15: The Planar Alliance

## Session Title and Summary
The players continue their investigation into the planar connection, discovering that entities from another plane have formed alliances with other dark forces. They must confront this while dealing with powerful planar threats.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the planar alliances
- Discover what these entities are trying to achieve
- Defend against powerful planar beings
