# Session 5: The Shadow Cult's Rise

## Session Title and Summary
The players return to Sunward village, finding that the situation has escalated. A shadow cult has emerged from the cave system, and they're recruiting followers throughout the region.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the rise of a shadow cult
- Discover their connection to the strange lights
- Defend against the cult's growing influence
