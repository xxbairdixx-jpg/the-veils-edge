# Session 13: The Planar Connection

## Session Title and Summary
The players have successfully dealt with the Void Nomads, but a new threat emerges - entities from another plane of existence. They must investigate this while dealing with powerful planar beings.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the connection to other planes
- Discover what these entities are doing in our world
- Defend against powerful planar beings

## Detailed Encounter Descriptions
