# Session 4: The Void's Whisper

## Session Title and Summary
The players continue their investigation into the cave system, discovering that the whispers are connected to a larger threat. They must confront this threat while dealing with increasing danger.

## Session Objectives
- Continue exploring the deeper parts of the cave system
- Investigate the source of the mysterious whispers
- Defend against multiple waves of goblin attacks

## Detailed Encounter Descriptions
