# Session 10: The Nomad's Stronghold

## Session Title and Summary
The players investigate the Void Nomads' activities, discovering their main stronghold in a nearby mountain range. They must infiltrate this stronghold while dealing with more powerful enemies.

## Session Objectives
- Investigate the Void Nomads' main stronghold
- Discover what they're doing there
- Defend against powerful nomadic raiders

## Detailed Encounter Descriptions
